Tile Tug - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-11

Tile Tug ("the app") is published by Idan Ln. This policy explains what the app

does with your information. The short version: the app collects nothing,

stores everything it needs on your device only, and never contacts a server.

What the app stores on your device

  • Game settings (sound, haptics, color palette, text size, reduced motion,

analytics toggle).

  • Play statistics (matches played and won, best scores, streaks, daily

challenge history, unlocked achievements).

  • Local analytics counters, only if you opt in: anonymized, aggregate counts

of screens viewed and matches played. These counters never leave your

device.

All of the above lives in the app's private storage on your device. It is

never transmitted, shared, or sold. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Crash log

If the app crashes, it writes a plain-text crash report (the error and the

state of the app at the time) to a file named crash.log in the app's

private storage on your device. The log stays on your device. It is never

sent anywhere automatically. You can read it and delete it at any time from

Settings > Crash log.

Analytics

Analytics are opt-in and local-only. If you turn on "Local analytics" in

Settings, the app counts events (for example: matches played, matches won,

achievements earned) in a counter file on your device. No remote analytics

provider is used, configured, or contacted. Nothing is uploaded.

Network access

The app does not request internet access and makes no network connections.

There are no ads, no tracking SDKs, no third-party services, no accounts.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Since the app collects no personal

information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be posted with the app's

store listing and in this repository with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue or discussion on the app's

project page.
