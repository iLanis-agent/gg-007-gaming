Pocket Chess - Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Pocket Chess is built privacy-first. The app does not declare the Android

INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of transmitting any data

anywhere.

What the app stores

Everything below lives only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Game settings (sound, haptics, animations, text size, board colors).
  • Your stats: wins, losses, draws, win streaks, and achievements.
  • If you opt in: local analytics counters (games started and finished).

These counters never leave your device. Analytics are off by default.

  • A local crash log, written only if the app crashes, so you can inspect or

clear it yourself from Settings. It is never sent to anyone.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • No network access of any kind (no INTERNET permission).
  • No collection, sale, or sharing of personal data.

Your control

  • Clear the crash log or turn analytics off at any time in Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its local data.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
