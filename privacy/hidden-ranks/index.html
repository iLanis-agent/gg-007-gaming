Hidden Ranks Privacy Policy

Hidden Ranks works offline. Match history, XP, ranks, settings, achievements, challenges, streaks, crash notes, and analytics preferences are stored only on the device. No personal data is sold or shared. Analytics and remote crash reporting are disabled; a future opt-in connection requires a provider choice and updated disclosure. Players can clear app data through Android settings.
